The breakdown of the real, irreal, and interwoven nature of time is where my practice begins. As portrayed in pieces such as Golden Voices (2024) and recalcitrante (2024), I use human anatomy and sacred geometry to exemplify how the divine intervenes through ancestral knowledge & history, and how emotions permeate epigenetically. These works are fragments of humanity, and highlight trauma’s toll on expression & body while maintaining the truth of celebration & the breakthrough that comes with finding one’s voice. The use of symbolism & abstraction of the body convey the weight of existence throughout my pieces. Due to my subject matter, intentional material exploration [& its failures], & the instinctual aspect of art-making is imperative to the release that comes through the process. My artistic practice is strengthened by ongoing conversations within my communities of how culture permeates into the contemporary through symbology and ritual, as well as what it means to be creating during times of hardship. In my solo show Corpos Recalcitrantes(2024), I displayed a collection of pieces alongside poetry that testify to how environmental disparities (both social & physical) have allowed me to interpret the world & be adaptable/proactive to its ever-changing conditions.
I use my activism & ongoing research into environmental studies & critical race theory to inform my interdisciplinary practice, which dissects [intergenerational] trauma, gender, sexuality, assimilation/reclamation, folklore, & institutional violence. Sede (2024) portrays where indigenous knowledge and reality clash, the radiant blues of the skin contrast with the melting of the environment due to acid rain. This critiques how the fundamental reciprocal relationships with nature are no longer reliable due to the devastating effects of mining. I use jarring colors & laboriously constructed materials that are associated with intricate histories, such as hair, bronze, photography, sugar, steel, as well as upcycled goods. 
By offering a material autonomy, I see a [trans]formation akin to mine, one where what is used is granted its opportunity to share its history, one where space is created to understand the delicate balance of existence. Throughout my photography, Dripping with Lace(2023) and ABSORBED SOLIP[CIS]M (2025), I mesh poetry and interpersonal power dynamics as a method of archiving the erasure & subsequent perseverance that have [trans]muted through my relationships. Song and prose, which have shaped my physical practice, have been heavily inspired by the lovingly recorded firsthand perspectives of iconic feminist/ womanist writers, such as but not limited to: Audre Lorde, Omi’Seke Tinsley, & Saidiya Hartman. Their writing invites people into what it is to create counterspaces that spread radical joy in the face of fear and hatred, which inspired me to create the AtlantiQ collective- a group where black artists can come together for discussion, to share resources, and sell their art for community mutual aid. Within these spaces that breed solidarity, we have been able to counter erasure & foster the boundless creativity that it takes to create culture from ashes. This exchange is what expands my work beyond the white cube and into every corner of existence- everything that axé moves through.
